ABOUT THE ORGANIZATION

This is the organizational chart of BRGY Luzviminda 1.

This organizational chart outlines the health services structure of Barangay Luzviminda 1 in Dasmariñas, Cavite, headed by Punong Barangay Jeffrey D. Galit. Healthcare operations are overseen by Evelina Y. Gulles (Committee on Health), supported by Nica Marie C. Lagazon (Midwife/Nurse) and Marites R. Gonzales (Barangay Nutrition Scholar). Directly assisting community medical efforts are Barangay Health Workers Falconery E. Abunales, Julie V. Nayola, Espiranza P. Ronquillo, and Norie C. Talinge.
